# Local reference host — Later required slices

These boundaries sequence work; they do not remove accepted B01–B15 requirements.
Each slice needs its own contract, implementation and executed evidence.

## External authority and private evaluation

### Optional signed acceptance (B12)

What: independent signer policy, domain/predecessor binding, rotation, revocation,
replay and recovery. Why later: first establish trusted operator/CI approval and
exact evidence binding. Trigger: enter B12 after the local authority lifecycle;
request specific approval for any paid signer/service.

### Operator-held challenges (B13)

What: private expectations, bounded candidate feedback and external query
accounting. Why later: B07 does not promise challenge secrecy or invent a query
budget. Trigger: enter B13 with the approved overfit-versus-ordinary fixture.

## Workloads and platforms

### Shared output, service and additional toolchain profiles (B09/B14)

What: representative multi-language construction and HTTP/browser/service
workloads with explicit readiness, reset and output ownership. Why later: the
first host has distinct per-probe artifact roots and pinned-image commands.
Trigger: B09's two-language pilots and each B14 workload contract, not speculative
claims based on the first POSIX-shell fixture.

### Stronger containment and Windows/non-POSIX (B14)

What: additional process/filesystem/network boundaries and actual platform tests.
Why later: Docker configuration and a macOS-hosted Linux execution do not prove
other platforms or host-administrator containment. Trigger: the B14 named-platform
slice after macOS/Linux evidence; obtain access/spending approval if needed.

### Hosted receipts/dashboard (B14)

What: multi-user receipt inspection while preserving local-only operation.
Why later: requires privacy, authority and workload design beyond a local delivery
directory. Trigger: the approved B14 hosted slice, with concrete cost review
before provisioning or paid usage. Research alone does not complete this slice.

## Customers and release

### Customer-specific integration and publication (B08/B10/B15)

What: OpenCode event handling, additional approved editor fragments and precise
release support claims. Why later: one adapter cannot define the protocol or
certify every host. Trigger: each customer compatibility/runtime matrix after the
shared contract; publication only with separate authority.
